## 2. Creative Direction: Editorial Technical Brutalism
Instead of looking like a generic SaaS template with purple glow, glassmorphism, and floating cards, DEZO adopts **Editorial Technical Brutalism**:
- **Physical Warm Paper Palette**: `#F5F3EE` creates a tactile, physical, book-like grounding. It feels permanent, honest, and easy to read.
- **Architectural Charcoal & Ochre**: Deep mineral charcoal `#0B0B0A` borders with restrained ochre gold `#B08D57` accents evoke traditional printing presses and architectural blueprints.
- **Typographic Dialectic**:
  - `Instrument Serif`: Editorial gravitas, artisanal mastery, and thoughtful intelligence.
  - `DM Sans`: Modern geometric precision and effortless UI legibility.
  - `JetBrains Mono`: High-density engineering rigor, transparent deliverables, and code-level truth.
- **Strict 0px Radius Geometry**: Confident, razor-sharp rectangular borders with hard-edge offset drop shadows.
